# Full HIS Process Simulator Update

This version expands the HIS Simulation Lab into a complete fictional hospital patient journey simulator.

## Added to HIS Simulator
- Full patient flow from patient arrival to discharge, transfer or death
- Patient outcome path selector: Discharge, Transfer, Death
- Death / expired patient workflow with pronouncement, death certificate sequence, body release and mortality coding readiness
- Transfer/referral workflow with destination, reason and referral note
- Discharge planning and discharge summary workflow
- Step-by-step clickable hospital process timeline
- People/departments involved in every stage
- HIS/EMR modules involved in every stage
- Required documents for every stage
- Practical student task and documentation risk for every stage

## Process stages now included
Patient arrival and registration, triage, insurance, admission decision, bed management, ward admission, clinical documentation, CPOE orders, laboratory, radiology, pharmacy, specialist consultation, surgery/procedure/anesthesia, daily progress and nursing notes, discharge planning, final clinical outcome, medical records review, medical coding and mortality coding, billing, archive and reporting.

## Educational note
All patient data and clinical scenarios are fictional and intended for university simulation and portfolio training only.
