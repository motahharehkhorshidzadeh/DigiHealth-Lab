# Hospital Statistics Forms & Medical Filing Disposition Update

Changes completed:

- Removed the previous Medical & Hospital Statistics content model.
- Added a new Hospital Statistics Forms & Analytics section.
- Added a downloadable Excel workbook with daily, monthly and annual statistical forms, sample completed data and formulas.
- Added category-based hospital statistics explanations: patient flow, bed utilization, mortality, surgery, OPD, emergency, diagnostic services, maternity/birth, HIM/records, coding/archive and analytics summary.
- Each statistical item explains frequency, definition, who completes it, where it is submitted and which Excel sheet contains the form.
- Updated Medical Filing retention/disposition content to focus on retention schedule, special records, permanent preservation and secure destruction register.
- Added guidance that retention periods vary by country/facility and must be verified against local laws, Ministry of Health rules and hospital policy.
