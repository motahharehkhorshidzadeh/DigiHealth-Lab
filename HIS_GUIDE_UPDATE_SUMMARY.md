# HIS Guide Update

Added a new platform section: **HIS Guide**.

Includes:
- HIS definition, purpose and scope
- HIS architecture, modules and system design
- Readiness before procurement
- RFI/RFP and vendor selection
- Contract and SLA safeguards
- Implementation and go-live roadmap
- Maintenance, support and upgrades
- Company/platform examples
- Security, privacy, data governance and interoperability
- Vendor selection scorecard

This section is intended for health information specialists, health IT teams, hospital managers, clinics and healthcare organizations preparing to choose or contract a Hospital Information System vendor.
