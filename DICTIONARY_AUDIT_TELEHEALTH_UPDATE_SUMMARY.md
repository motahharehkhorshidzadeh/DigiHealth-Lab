# Dictionary, Audit and Telehealth Update

This version expands DigiHealth Lab in three major areas:

## 1. Expanded Terminology Dictionary
- Added a much larger categorized dictionary with medical terminology, clinical specialties, signs and symptoms, diseases and conditions, procedures, surgical/service terms, medical abbreviations, diagnostic/lab/imaging terms, FHIR resources, medical technology terms, clinical documentation forms, ICD chapter ranges, ICD example codes, ICD-10-PCS root operations, CPT/HCPCS ranges and examples, LOINC examples, ATC medication classes, HIS/EMR modules, audit terms, mortality coding terms and telehealth/mHealth terms.
- The dictionary is designed as a large academic core dictionary and code-oriented terminology library. Official full code sets such as CPT and national drug product files should be verified through official/licensed sources in a production version.

## 2. Audit & Missing Docs Output
- The Audit page now shows why it is useful and what final result it produces.
- Added final audit outcome with record completeness score, risk level, open missing documents, critical follow-up items, coding readiness and recommended action.
- Added a copyable audit outcome report for student portfolio or instructor review.

## 3. Full Telehealth Simulator
- Rebuilt Telehealth Simulator as a complete workflow: pre-visit, check-in, consultation, RPM review, documentation and follow-up.
- Added virtual visit workstation, RPM data, privacy/environment note, care plan, outcome selection, telehealth documentation checklist, risk level and copyable telehealth report.
